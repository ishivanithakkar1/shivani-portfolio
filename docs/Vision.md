# Vision

To create a digital experience that reflects who I am as a creator.

Not just a software developer.

Not just a singer.

But someone who builds meaningful experiences through technology and creativity.

The goal is not to impress visitors with flashy animations or complex technology.

The goal is to leave them with one feeling:

"I want to work with her."

---

# Our Philosophy

We believe that beautiful software is not created by adding more.

It is created by removing everything that doesn't matter.

Every color.

Every animation.

Every word.

Every interaction.

Must have a purpose.

---

# Design Principles

## Quiet Confidence

The website should never feel loud.

Confidence doesn't need to shout.

---

## Elegant Technology

Technology should disappear into the experience.

Visitors shouldn't notice the framework.

They should notice the feeling.

---

## Curiosity Rewarded

Every scroll.

Every hover.

Every interaction.

Should reward curiosity.

The website should invite exploration instead of demanding attention.

---

## Human First

People don't hire code.

People hire people.

The website should communicate personality before technology.

---

## Less, But Better

If a section can be removed without changing the story...

It should be removed.
